Why Looking at the Stars Means Looking Back in Time

Watch on YouTube ↗Jason TV channel ↗

When you look at the stars, you see light that left them long before tonight. It is beautiful because it is alive with patterns, motion, and time on a scale we can barely imagine. When you look up at the night sky, you are seeing ancient light, you are looking into history, and you are remembering something deep inside you… that you are not separate from the cosmos.
