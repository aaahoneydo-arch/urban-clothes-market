Deuteronomy Explained: The Final Words of Moses

Watch on YouTube ↗Jason TV channel ↗

Deuteronomy is the fifth book of the Bible and the final chapter of Moses' leadership. In this episode, we break down the major events, laws, and speeches Moses delivered before his death. From the retelling of the Ten Commandments to blessings, curses, and his last vision of the Promised Land, this film summarizes the entire Book of Deuteronomy in one powerful documentary.
