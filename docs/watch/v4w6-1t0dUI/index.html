The Global History of Slavery – A Simon Podcast

Watch on YouTube ↗Jason TV channel ↗

From ancient empires to modern times, slavery has shaped the world in ways many have never realized. In this powerful podcast, Simon explores the brutal reality of human bondage across continents and centuries — with facts, not opinions. 🎙️ This episode covers the full timeline, starting from the earliest records of slavery in Mesopotamia and Egypt, through the African, European, Islamic, and transatlantic slave trades, all the way to modern-day human trafficking.
