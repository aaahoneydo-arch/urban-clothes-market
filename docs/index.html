A guide to the collection

Finding a graphic that works on the garment

A crowned phoenix framed by dark roses reads differently on a hoodie than on a mug. Start with the artwork in the design catalog, then open its style list. Every listed variation names the actual garment or accessory and links to that item on Spreadshirt. A design with a hoodie available may have a different set of shirts or accessories from the design beside it.

Gothic streetwear, crests and animal artwork

Look for the details that distinguish each illustration. The Self Made phoenix stands over a blacksmith’s anvil. The Family direwolves guard a silver tree, while the New York gargoyle collection draws on stone cathedral sculpture. Browse by design to compare the artwork, or use the hoodie collection to find garments first.

Choosing sizes, colors and gifts

A men’s premium hoodie, a unisex shirt and a youth garment have different measurements. Open the seller’s exact product page to compare its size chart and choose an available color. For gifts that do not need a clothing size, browse home goods and accessories for the product types available in the current collection.

Books beyond the clothing collection

Jason Coleman also writes fantasy, science fiction, espionage and nonfiction. Browse the books to compare the available Kindle, print and audiobook editions. Where a title has a trailer or narrated graphic novel, the book page links to the matching video below the purchase options.

Finding a shirt, hoodie or accessory in the same design

Open the design page and use its style filter. Typing hoodie narrows the list to hoodies using that artwork. Typing mug, shirt or bag shows matching entries where the seller offers them. The price beside each style refers to that specific item. The seller page shows the print placement, available colors and current delivery options.

Discover the stories behind the books

The collection also includes vampire fiction, post-apocalyptic survival stories and nonfiction by Jason Coleman. A reader looking for a narrated comic can open Draevan or The Dark Versus Light Legions, watch the matching adaptation, and return to the Amazon edition links. Readers can also browse Jason TV Shorts or search the full video archive by title.
