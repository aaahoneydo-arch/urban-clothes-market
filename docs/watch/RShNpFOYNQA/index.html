The Origins of Race and Language: What Made Us Different?

Watch on YouTube ↗Jason TV channel ↗

Why do humans look so different across the world? Why do we speak in over 7,000 languages? And how did we go from one common ancestor… to thousands of tribes, cultures, and nations? This cinematic documentary explores the evolutionary, environmental, and historical roots of race and language. From ancient migrations out of Africa to isolated island communities and global empires, you’ll discover the untold story of how humanity diversified — and how deeply connected we really are.
