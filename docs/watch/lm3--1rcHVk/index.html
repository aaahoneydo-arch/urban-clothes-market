Step back into 1936 and watch one of the most infamous films ever made. Originally released as a serious anti-marijuana cautionary film, Reefer Madness has since become one of the world's most famous cult classics. What was once intended as a shocking warning is now remembered for its dramatic storytelling, unforgettable performances, and fascinating place in American film history.
