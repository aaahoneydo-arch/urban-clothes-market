U.S. National Debt per Person | What the October 2026 Figure Means

Watch on YouTube ↗Jason TV channel ↗

Forty trillion dollars. Almost too big to picture. America’s total federal debt reached $40.24 trillion as of October 2, 2026. Divided across the population, that’s roughly $120,000 for every man, woman, and child. That figure illustrates the scale of federal debt. It is not a personal bill or an amount each American individually owes. Interest on that debt takes money from the federal budget that could serve other priorities. We deserve a serious conversation about the tradeoffs—and a credible plan.
