Mount Tambora and the Year Without a Summer

Watch on YouTube ↗Jason TV channel ↗

In 1815, a volcano erupted so violently it changed the world. Mount Tambora exploded in Indonesia. It was one of the largest eruptions in recorded history. The blast killed tens of thousands instantly. But the ash didn’t stay local. It shot so high, it reached the stratosphere. Massive clouds of ash and sulfur circled the Earth. Temperatures dropped across the globe.
