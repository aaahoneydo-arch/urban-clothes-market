🔥King Leopold II: The Butcher of Congo (Full Documentary)🔥

This is the complete story of King Leopold II — told with accuracy, clarity, and no outside interpretations. King Leopold II of Belgium created a private empire in the Congo, driven by greed and maintained through terror. Between 10 and 20 million Congolese men, women, and children died under his brutal regime. This documentary tells the horrifying truth of what happened in the Congo Free State, from the forced labor and mutilations to the international campaign that finally exposed the atrocities.
