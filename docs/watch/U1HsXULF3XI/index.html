Historical correction: this Short combines the stories of two South African baboons. Jackie served as a mascot with South African troops in World War I. Jack helped the signalman James Wide operate railway signals at Uitenhage in the late 1800s. The military and railway episodes belonged to different animals. 💥 Loyal. Brave.
