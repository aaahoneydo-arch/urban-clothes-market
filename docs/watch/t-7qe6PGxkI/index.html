What if the movie once called "the worst film ever made" was actually one of the greatest cult classics in cinema history? Released in 1959, Plan 9 from Outer Space has become one of the most recognizable science fiction films ever made. Directed by Ed Wood, this legendary cult classic blends flying saucers, aliens, zombies, suspense, and unforgettable performances into one of the most entertaining movies of its era.
