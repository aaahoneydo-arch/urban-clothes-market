What the Bible and the Quran Say About Jesus’ Crucifixion

Watch on YouTube ↗Jason TV channel ↗

The Bible and the Quran both tell stories of creation, prophets, and judgment — but some differences are deeper than most realize. One of the biggest? What each says about the fate of Jesus. In Christianity, Jesus is crucified and resurrected. In Islam, the Quran says he was never crucified — it only appeared so. Two sacred books. One story told two ways.
