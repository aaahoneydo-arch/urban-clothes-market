🎥 The Greatest Sieges in History

Watch on YouTube ↗Jason TV channel ↗

War isn't always fought on open ground. Sometimes, the fate of entire civilizations is decided behind stone walls, in silence, and over months or even years. This documentary explores the greatest sieges in history — from Troy to Tenochtitlán, Constantinople to Leningrad. These are the stories of bravery, starvation, deception, and endurance that changed the world forever.
