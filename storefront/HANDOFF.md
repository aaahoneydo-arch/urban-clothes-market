# How product checkout was repaired

Both sites use one merchant catalog. A merchandise row is published only after Spreadshirt identifies the Urban Clothes Market creator, design identifier, exact sellable SKU, available product type and appearance, and an orderable USD offer. Buy links use the URL returned by the offer itself. A product photo, an HTTP success code, or a public design status alone cannot establish a match.

The original Phoenix hoodie linked to a myspreadshop.com page that returned 404. Its live marketplace offer showed the same design on the men's premium hoodie at $56.99. The historical site advertised $30.99. Blanket hostname replacement also failed on designs for which Spreadshirt returned a different product or had no stock. The prior build used several independent copies of the catalog and could reuse old generated pages.

The November 9 release checked 22,075 historical merchandise SKUs and traversed all 233 category and pagination pages linked by the public Spreadshirt designer catalog. The four main designer pages contained 227 different designs. Across the historical and public seller records, 27,242 unique candidate SKUs were considered. The final release includes 24,796 verified seller offers. The remaining 2,446 records are withheld: 209 returned 404, 1,962 did not return the expected seller/design/variant identity, and 275 did not confirm an available appearance or size. In 245 supplemental checks the seller provided an available color for the exact same item; only the current confirmed offer and image were retained.

Amazon book editions came from the public author page and were reconciled with the live KDP bookshelf. The author page showed separate retail listings for formats of one graphic novel. They are grouped under one work. TIFF Book Three: Still Here was located on the live bookshelf and its Amazon Kindle order page. The two sites offer 22 works in 53 edition-specific links.

The public Jason TV channel inventory included 597 videos, 795 Shorts, and 99 livestreams. Each has a watch page and a direct YouTube link. Merchandise and books precede video discovery on both homepages. Videos about specific books or merchandise link back to the matching catalog entries.

The live publishing repositories are aaahoneydo-arch/urban-clothes-market and aaahoneydo-arch/jason-coleman-media-network, both on main/docs. The code, imports, checks, and shared data live in the former repository's storefront folder. Before publishing, run the shared builder and link checker for both domains. Check release.json on both live domains and verify a purchase link, an old ad URL, a book edition, and a video. The website-hosted merchandise feeds contain Spreadshirt offers only and require matching seller imagery. Merchant account submissions and campaign management are separate.

Search Console also reported previously indexed full product titles where an older build truncated the slug at 60 characters. Known full and truncated historical advertising routes are generated from the original catalog and recorded URL map. An unknown historic spelling can recover only an unambiguous matching seller SKU.
