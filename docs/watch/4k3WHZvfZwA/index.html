Hatshepsut’s Expedition to Punt | Calm History for Sleep | 5 Hours

Watch on YouTube ↗Jason TV channel ↗

Explore Hatshepsut’s expedition to the Land of Punt in this calm ancient Egyptian history episode for sleep and relaxation. Discover the ships, trade and temple reliefs associated with the journey. This episode explores the painted reliefs of Deir el-Bahari, Kathryn Bard and Rodolfo Fattovich's excavations of the harbor caves at Mersa Gawasis where cedar planks and coiled ropes survived, and Karnak's botanical garden. Across twelve full-length chapters covering Africa, we explore what happened, why it.
