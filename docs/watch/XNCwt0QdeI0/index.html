Explore the Uluburun shipwreck and Bronze Age maritime trade in this calm history episode for sleep and relaxation. Discover the ship’s cargo and the connected world it reveals. This episode examines George Bass and Cemal Pulak's underwater excavation of ten tons of Cypriot copper ingots, one ton of tin, cobalt-blue glass ingots, terebinth resin in Canaanite jars, African ebony, and Baltic amber.
