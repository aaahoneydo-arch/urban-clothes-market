# Production storefront

The live GitHub Pages publication is main/docs. Urban Clothes Market and Jason TV Marketplace share the source and catalog in aaahoneydo-arch/urban-clothes-market, in the storefront directory. Keep checkout on the actual Amazon edition or official Spreadshirt seller item. Do not add other merchandise providers, storefront homepages, search-result substitutes, or locally invented SKUs to the shopping catalog.

Use storefront/README.md for imports, seller checks, building, and publishing. The original root HTML and older catalog programs are historical and do not publish the current websites. Preserve the main/docs source setting, custom domain, verification files, ad landing routes, and the complete public Jason TV video and Shorts archive. Check both domains after publishing.
