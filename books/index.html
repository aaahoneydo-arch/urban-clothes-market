Dixon's Journal: Book One — A House Beneath The Ash

After the Yellowstone Supervolcano, One Handyman's Notebook Records What Survival Actually Costs

When Yellowstone suffers a catastrophic caldera eruption, eastern Kentucky handyman Dixon Hale doesn't become an invincible action hero—he becomes a father trying to keep a basement stove vented, a gravity spring flowing, and a ledger of dwindling meals balanced for his wife Mara and children Wren and Owen. Written in first-person journal entries across 793 days under the ash, Book One of Dixon's Journal is a gripping, grounded survival novel where practical skill, tools, community, and human loss matter.

A Beautiful Alibi

The Borrowed Names, Book One — Near-Future Espionage, Identity & Deception
